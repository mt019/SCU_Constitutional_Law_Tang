2024 FGS Chan and Pure Land
March 2, 2024 3:30 AM
星雲大師云:「解在一切佛法，行在禪淨共修。」
 藉由淨土，信願念佛，淨水當下世界。 透過禪修，集中意志，收攝散亂身心。活動宗旨：秉持佛教無緣大慈、同體大悲之精神，為祈求新冠疫情病毒早日消弭、世界和平，特此舉辦線上祈福法會。
主辦單位：佛光山寺
承辦單位：國際佛光會中華總會 
協辦單位：多倫多佛光山
法會時間: 2024年3月2、3日(六、日) 下午4點30分至7點 (台灣時間)
參與方式: 可於家中搭海青縵衣，觀看「人間衛視」或YouTube「國際佛光會」https://www.youtube.com/c/BLIAWHQ 頻道，精進修持、參與盛會。
Venerable Master Hsing Yun: “Understand the Dharma; cultivate the joint practice of Chan and Pure Land.”
Recite the Buddha's name with faith and vow through Pure Land practice, purifying the world in the here and now; concentrate the mind by practicing Chan, focusing the chaotic body and mind.
‍
Objective: This Dharma service for blessings is conducted in upholding Buddhism’s spirit of unconditional compassion and loving-kindness, praying for an early end of the pandemic and for world peace.
Organizer: Fo Guang Shan Monastery
Institution-in-charge: BLIA Chunghua
Co-organizers: Fo Guang Shan Temple of Toronto
Date: March 2-3, 2024 (Sat-Sun) // 4:30-7:00 PM (Taiwan Time)
To participate: 
Wear the haiqing and precept robes to get online to watch BLTV or BLIA https://www.youtube.com/c/BLIAWHQ on YouTube, participating in the service for diligent practice.
