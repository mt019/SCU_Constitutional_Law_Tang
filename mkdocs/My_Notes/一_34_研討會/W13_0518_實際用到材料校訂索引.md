## 三、Fourie 主文校訂重點

THE ORDER 中課堂實際逐句看的部分：

- `The application for leave to appeal ... is granted.`
- `The application for leave to cross-appeal ... is granted.`
- `The common law definition of marriage is declared to be inconsistent with the Constitution and invalid to the extent that it does not permit same-sex couples to enjoy the status and the benefits coupled with responsibilities it accords to heterosexual couples.`
- `The declaration of invalidity is suspended for twelve months from the date of this judgment to allow Parliament to correct the defect.`
- `The application by the Lesbian and Gay Equality Project and Eighteen Others for direct access is granted.`
- `The omission from section 30(1) of the Marriage Act 25 of 1961 after the words "or husband" of the words "or spouse" is declared to be inconsistent with the Constitution`
- `Should Parliament not correct the defects within this period, Section 30(1) ... will forthwith be read as including the words "or spouse" after the words "or husband" as they appear in the marriage formula.`
- `Langa CJ, Moseneke DCJ, Mokgoro J, Ngcobo J, Skweyiya J, Van der Westhuizen J and Yacoob J concur in the judgment of Sachs J.`

## 四、逐字稿常見誤辨字校正表

- `sux`、`sex justice` → `Sachs J`
- `A Missing Curie` → `amici curiae`
- `Direct Assist Iscranti` → `direct access is granted`
- `Fouhy`、`Fury`、`Furhi`、`Furhi case` → `Fourie`
- `Bongnia`、`Bongfais` → `Bonthuys`
- `Mary` / `Marié` 相關處以判決為準：`Marié Adriaana Fourie`
- `Cicilia` → `Cecelia Johanna Bonthuys`
- `or sparse` → `or spouse`
- `Invertebrate` → `invalidity`
- `emission`、`審略` → `omission`／「省略」
- `CCD for Earth` → `CCT 10/05`
- `Declaration of Invertebrate` → `declaration of invalidity`
- `Public to have its say`：課堂口譯為「公眾有說話／表達意見的機會」
- `the period of suspension of invalidity`：可校為「違憲失效宣告的暫緩期間」
